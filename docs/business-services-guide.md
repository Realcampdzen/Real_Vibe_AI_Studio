## Lead Qualification Checklist

For any incoming request, collect:

- objective: what business result is needed;
- deliverables: assets, screens, videos, files, code, integrations;
- target platforms: site, Telegram, VK, Instagram, YouTube, marketplace, app, internal team;
- deadlines and launch date;
- available materials: text, brand, design, references, product photos, code repository;
- constraints: budget, legal/medical/financial limits, approval process;
- first safe step: audit, concept, prototype, one module, one video, one content pack.

## Pricing Rules

Use these rules in proposals:

- Do not promise a final price before scope is clear.
- Give a safe first stage when scope is uncertain.
- Separate audit/concept/prototype from full production when risk is high.
- For development, price by module and integration complexity.
- For content/media, price by final deliverables, number of versions, editing depth, deadline, and source-material quality.
- For AI agents, price by knowledge-base prep, safety boundaries, integrations, evaluation tests, and onboarding.
